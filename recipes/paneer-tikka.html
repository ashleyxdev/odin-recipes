Paneer Tikka

Smoky, Spiced, and Char-Grilled – India's Favourite Vegetarian Starter

Paneer Tikka is a classic North Indian appetiser where thick cubes of fresh cottage cheese are marinated in a bold yoghurt-based spice mixture and then grilled or roasted at high heat until beautifully charred on the edges. It draws from the same tandoor-cooking tradition as its meat counterparts, delivering the same smoky, intense flavour in a completely vegetarian form.

The marinade is the heart of the dish — hung curd mixed with ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri red chilli for colour, garam masala, cumin, carom seeds, and a touch of mustard oil for that distinctly North Indian depth. Chunks of capsicum and onion are skewered alongside the paneer, picking up the same spiced char and adding textural contrast. The result is a visually striking plate of jewel-toned pieces with a slightly smoky crust and a soft, creamy interior.

Paneer Tikka is most commonly served as a starter or snack, paired with mint chutney and sliced onion rings. It works equally well made on a gas flame grill, in an oven under the broiler, or on a stovetop grill pan — accessible to every kitchen, yet always impressive on the table.

Ingredients:

  • 400g paneer, cut into thick cubes
  • 1 large capsicum, cut into squares
  • 1 large onion, cut into squares
  • 1 cup thick hung curd (yoghurt)
  • 1½ tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 1½ tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (or any neutral oil)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • Chaat masala for finishing
  • Mint chutney and lemon wedges for serving

Steps:

  1. In a large bowl, combine hung curd, ginger-garlic paste, all dry spices, mustard oil, lemon juice, and salt. Mix into a smooth marinade.
  2. Add paneer cubes, capsicum, and onion pieces. Coat thoroughly and refrigerate for at least 1–2 hours (overnight for best results).
  3. Preheat your oven grill/broiler to maximum heat, or heat a grill pan until very hot.
  4. Thread paneer, capsicum, and onion alternately onto skewers.
  5. Grill for 10–12 minutes, turning halfway, until the edges are charred and the marinade is cooked through.
  6. If using an oven, place on a wire rack and broil on the highest rack, turning once.
  7. Sprinkle chaat masala over the hot tikka immediately after grilling.
  8. Serve with mint chutney, sliced onion rings, and lemon wedges.
